# Chord Chart for Android: privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Chord Chart works out the chords, beats, bars and key of a song entirely on your phone.
This policy covers the Chord Chart app published on Google Play
(package `io.github.shanikn.chordchart`).

## What the app collects

Nothing. Chord Chart has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and
the Google Play version doesn't have permission to use the internet at all.

- **Songs you open** are read from your phone only to analyse them. They are never
  uploaded, copied elsewhere, or shared with anyone.
- **The microphone** is used only while you're on the Listen screen, after you allow
  it: the app records the song you play, analyses the recording on the phone, then
  discards it. Only the chart is kept. No recording is saved or sent anywhere.
- **Charts** (the result of an analysis: song title, file name, chords, beats, key,
  tempo and when it was analysed) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone,
  so you can open them again. Delete one from the app's home screen, or remove them all
  by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android's settings. They are not
  included in Android's cloud backups, though some phones may copy app data, charts
  included, when you move to a new phone with a cable or a direct transfer.

## Sharing

No data is sent to the developer or to any third party, because none leaves your phone.

## Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
